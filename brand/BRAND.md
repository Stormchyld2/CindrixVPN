# CindrixVPN brand

Sibling of CindrixOps: near-black ground, bold spaced monospace wordmark, one bright accent
(blue here, gold in CindrixOps).

| Role | Hex |
| --- | --- |
| Background | `#0E1317` |
| Panel | `#121A21` / `#18212A` |
| Text | `#E6EEF5` |
| Accent | `#3DA5FF` |
| Accent deep (buttons, light theme) | `#1F78E0` |
| Muted / tagline | `#7FA3B8` |

- Wordmark: `CINDRIX` in text color, `VPN` in accent, bold monospace, wide letter-spacing.
- Tagline: `YOUR TRAFFIC. YOUR RULES.` (placeholder, subject to change)
- Icon: shield outline with a flame inside (flame ties to CindrixOps).
- Status colors stay semantic: green = protected, red = unprotected.

The app theme lives in
`src/Client/Common/ProtonVPN.Client.Common.UI/Styles/Colors.xaml` and `Brushes.xaml`.
